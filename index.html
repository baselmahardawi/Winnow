About Winnow

How matching works

Records are compared on DOI and PMID first, then on title, year and first author. Titles are compared without capitals, accents or punctuation, using character-pair similarity. Only certain matches are removed. Everything else that looks similar is flagged for you.

What to know

Winnow is being validated, so check its results before relying on them. It reads RIS, PubMed (.nbib), Web of Science (plain text, tab-delimited, RIS or BibTeX), BibTeX and CSV files. Check the Removed tab and the flagged pairs before relying on a result. Trial registry entries are flagged when their title matches a publication. The optional exclusion of study types uses only explicit statements in the title, abstract or database labels, and every excluded record is listed with its evidence so you can restore it.

Privacy and use

Your files are read in your browser and never uploaded. The saved session stays in this browser only, so use Clear saved session when you finish a review. Winnow is released under the MIT licence.

Records are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Matching order: DOI, PMID, exact normalised title with year and first author, then fuzzy title similarity (bigram Dice). Anything short of a certain match is flagged, never removed silently. Version 1.0.0.
